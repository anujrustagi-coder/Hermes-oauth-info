Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. About this application

Hermes Personal Assistant is a privately operated AI assistant for personal productivity, research, and document management. It is intended for use by its owner, not as a public service.

2. Google data accessed

With the account owner's authorisation, Hermes may access permitted Google Drive files and other Google Workspace data covered by the OAuth permissions granted to the application.

3. How data is used

Google data is used to fulfil tasks requested by the authorised user, such as locating, reading, analysing and organising documents.

4. AI processing and disclosure

Relevant information may be processed using third-party AI model providers to perform requested tasks. This may involve transmitting selected content to those providers.

5. Data storage and security

Authentication credentials and application working files are maintained within the owner's controlled computing environment. Retrieved information may be retained in assistant workspaces, logs or task outputs. Access is restricted through the configured Google account permissions and local controls.

6. Data sharing

The application is not intended to sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Data may be shared with service providers as necessary to perform authorised tasks.

7. Revoking access

The account owner may revoke Google access through Google Account security settings and delete locally retained data.
